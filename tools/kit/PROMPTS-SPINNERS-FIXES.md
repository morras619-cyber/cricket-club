```

1. The red ball is in his RIGHT hand, in the fingertips, in every pose until release, and it is always fully visible:

   arm. It is NEVER held flat and straight out like a wing.
5. His body always faces down the pitch. Turn the shoulders side-on by at most 40 degrees. Never turn the whole body to
   face the left or right edge of the picture.
6. After release draw NO ball anywhere: not in the hand, not in the air.
7. Same man, same height and same kit in all five poses, standing on the same ground line.
```
